Privacy

AppHub does not track individual visitors. We only use aggregate download counts.

Last updated August 19, 2026

What AppHub collects

AppHub records aggregate AppImage download totals published by release providers. These totals generate the trending lists. They are not used to identify you or build a profile about you.

What AppHub does not collect

AppHub does not have user accounts, advertising, tracking cookies, fingerprinting, or client-side analytics. We do not sell or share personal data.

Hosting and downloads

AppHub is hosted by GitHub Pages. App downloads are provided by their publishers, including through GitHub. Those services process requests under their own privacy policies. GitHub's processing is governed by the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Data retention

AppHub keeps aggregate download snapshots for trending calculations. We do not store individual visit or download records. Hosting and release providers control the retention of data they process.

External links

App pages may link to third-party websites and source repositories. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Changes to this policy

Any material changes will be published here with an updated date.
